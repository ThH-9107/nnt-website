# Content Architecture — Portfolio

## 1. Primary principle

The portfolio is not organized as a list of résumé categories.

The October 15, 2026 review preserves this architecture. Public prose now emphasizes distinct personal contributions and experiences, with metrics consolidated and limitations explained where they affect interpretation. Community service and theatre retain their own identities alongside the research and science education.

The main conceptual navigation is based on **scales of attention**:

1. **IMAGE**
2. **ORGANISM**
3. **LEARNER**
4. **COMMUNITY**
5. **AUDIENCE**

Secondary action verbs:
- IMAGE — *See*
- ORGANISM — *Test*
- LEARNER — *Share*
- COMMUNITY — *Serve*
- AUDIENCE — *Express*

The primary labels should be the **scale names**, not the verbs.

## 5. Distinct narrative mechanics by project

### Medical AI
Mechanic: **evolution of the research question**

Suggested structure:
1. why the question started
2. 2023 prototype
3. 2024 inconclusive augmentation work
4. move to medical imaging
5. self-supervised learning
6. current method
7. experiment scale
8. current result + trade-off
9. manuscript
10. what remains uncertain

Visual language:
- grid
- image / representation
- controlled comparisons
- research-path branching

### Phytophthora
Mechanic: **field → lab → revise**

Suggested sequence:
FARM → SYMPTOM → SAMPLE → CULTURE → OBSERVE → REVISE

Include experimental loop:
sample → isolate → inspect → inconsistent → revise ↺

Do not manufacture a final result.

### MoleBio

Before:
- ~16-member predecessor

After:
- 20-member hands-on MoleBio
- 36 sessions
- ~48h
- lab skills
- workshops
- outreach

Photo-heavy.

Listen/Practice retain adjacent contrasting editorial panels. Beyond is directly connected underneath with two authentic, separate laboratory-learning experiences. It is conceptual, not chronological. Detailed workshops and projects follow; the ending is “What I Couldn't Carry Forward,” using Thanh's approved iGEM/succession account.

The website should add what the résumé cannot show:
- chronology
- decisions
- uncertainty
- personal contribution
- photos
- diagrams
- failed / inconclusive work
- reflections
- evidence
- process

Avoid copying résumé bullets verbatim into long pages.
